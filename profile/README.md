## 🚀 Introduction
Editing long recordings by hand is slow, and most of the work is repetitive: cutting the silences, trimming the pauses, and lining up clips. Auto Video Editor for Windows automates exactly that. As an auto AI video editor, it analyzes your footage, removes dead air and long pauses, cuts at natural moments, and assembles the pieces into a ready-to-review sequence. It suits creators, teachers, and teams who record often and want the mechanical editing done automatically before they add the finishing touches.

## ✨ Highlights

### Automatic editing
- **AI auto-cut** — finds sensible cut points and slices your clips without manual scrubbing.
- **Silence and pause removal** — strips out quiet gaps, dead air, and drawn-out pauses.
- **Fast assembly** — pieces the surviving clips together into a complete rough cut in minutes.

### Staying in control
- **Automatic arrangement** — sequences clips into a coherent order you can review at a glance.
- **Manual overrides** — adjust, re-cut, or reorder anything the AI decided when you want the final call.
